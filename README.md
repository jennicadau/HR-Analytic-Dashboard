### Problem Statement

PwC Australia is a leading professional services corporate, promoting diversity and inclusion in workplace. Analysing the dataset to find insights and provide suggestions to improve current situation. 

- What is the current challenges/gap in hiring and promotion?
- What is the performance of male and female?
- How is turnover rate in the corporate at the moment?
- Where is the improvement needed to be focused?

### **Business Objective**

The purpose of this project is to create automated dashboards which allows HR managers aware of the current gap in hiring and work promotion, turnover insight and performance across the board to support the hiring process and employee development assistance. 

### **Tools**  

Excel, PowerBI, Dax, M-language. 


# **The Approach and Process**

I structured my work as a sequence of problem-solving steps:

**Human Resource Management** 

- Find the gender gap across job level and department.
- Find the gender gap in hiring and turnover.
- Track work promotion comparison between FY20 and FY21 (recent year).

**Performance Insight:**

- How performance difference is between men and women?
- Find the gender gap performance in job level and department.

**Other analysis:**

- How age group is reflected in hiring process?
- Where is distinct age groups situated in turnover rate?

# **End Results and Recommendations**

### Key Insights

- **Sales & marking department** experienced significant higher men hired than women hired, which is **almost double.**
- The percentage of men hired **is nearly 2 times** of women hired percentage.
- High rate, **10.2%**, of employee got **promoted**  in FY21.
- The previous year, FY20, has a healthy **turnover rate at 10%**, however, it spikes in **age group 50-59** at **63%**.
- Women does not get hired if they are **50+ years old**, while **men get hired 100%**.
- **Performance rating is equal** between men and women is across the board.

### **Strategic Business Recommendations**

- **Gender equity:** promote and encourage gender equity in the workforce, as well as design specific employee assistance programs to underrepresented gender.
- **Employee retention:** introduce flexible working arrangements, age-inclusive programs and career change programs.
- **Advanced technology:** implement mandatory age-blind, gender-blind and hidden name to improve recruitment equity.
- **Awareness:** conduct and develop leadership training and HR on intersectional bias (age & gender).
- **Investigation:**
    - Design and collect surveys to find the root problems.
